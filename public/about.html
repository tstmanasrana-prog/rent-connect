Our Story

Ending the Broker Mafia in Baripada.

RentConnect was born from a simple frustration: Why should finding a home be so expensive and complicated? For too long, brokers have controlled the market, charging high fees for simply sharing a phone number.

"We believe that information should be free and direct."

As a **Soul Shift Media** initiative, RentConnect is built to empower tenants and landlords to talk directly. No middleman, no hidden charges, and absolutely no brokerage.

Verified Only

Every property on our platform is manually verified by our team to ensure 100% accuracy.
